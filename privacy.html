Privacy policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Who this applies to

Business Engine is a private program used only by the owner of one YouTube channel. The only Google account that signs in to it is the channel owner's own account.

What it accesses

  • Through the YouTube Data API: uploading videos to, and managing videos on, the owner's own channel.
  • Through the YouTube Analytics API: the owner's own channel statistics (for example views and watch time).

It does not access, collect or store personal information about viewers or any other people, and it does not read anyone else's channel or account data.

How data is stored

The sign-in token and the channel's statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to any other server except Google's own APIs, and they are never sold, shared or used for advertising.

YouTube API Services

Business Engine uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's security settings: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Removing access stops all uploads and statistics reading. Stored statistics can be deleted by the owner at any time.
